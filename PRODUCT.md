# BookLeaf Publishing — Product Definition

## Vision
The Author Support & Communication Portal is the central nervous system for BookLeaf Publishing's 22,000+ catalog titles and 1,200+ monthly book releases across India and the US. It eliminates manual triage bottlenecks and inconsistent communication by marrying asynchronous BullMQ auto-triage with on-demand Valkey-cached AI drafting and real-time author-ops communication.

## Target Audience & Personas
1. **The Author (Creator)**:
   - Needs transparent, line-by-line royalty accounting (80/20 split, quarterly cycle, payout threshold).
   - Needs immediate peace of mind regarding book status (Cover Design, Typesetting, Printing, Distribution).
   - Expects respectful, empathetic, and specific answers—not robotic generic fluff.
2. **Operations Team Member (Editor / Support Specialist)**:
   - Needs high-throughput triage: urgent and old unresolved tickets must command immediate attention.
   - Needs rich context upfront (author bank status, book sales, royalties pending, distribution channels).
   - Needs rapid AI drafting with 1-click review, editing, and cache invalidation.

## Tone & Register
- **Atmosphere**: Prestigious, trustworthy, editorial, warm, and rigorously professional.
- **Voice**: Empathetic partner, not bureaucratic corporate deflection. Takes immediate ownership of errors and specifies exact timelines (48-hour resolution, 5-7 business days reprint).
